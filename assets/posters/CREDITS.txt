Classic movie posters bundled in this folder are in the PUBLIC DOMAIN and were
sourced from Wikimedia Commons, where each file is tagged "Public domain".
Attribution is not legally required for public-domain works, but the source is
noted here for transparency. To view a file's source page, search its title at:

    https://commons.wikimedia.org/

The bundled thumbnails were downloaded via the Wikimedia Commons API at ~500px
width. They depict classic films (mostly 1900s-1960s) whose posters are in the
public domain -- including works by Chaplin, Keaton, Murnau, Lang, Eisenstein
and Melies, plus non-renewed studio-era and B-movie posters.

All image files in this folder are tagged "Public domain" on Wikimedia Commons.
